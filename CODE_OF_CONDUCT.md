# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This organization values direct, respectful, technically serious collaboration.

Expected behavior:

- discuss changes in concrete engineering terms
- challenge ideas without attacking people
- keep reviews focused on behavior, risk, and maintainability
- prefer clarity over posturing

Unacceptable behavior:

- harassment or discrimination
- personal attacks
- deliberate disruption
- publishing private or sensitive information without permission

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors that violate this standard.
